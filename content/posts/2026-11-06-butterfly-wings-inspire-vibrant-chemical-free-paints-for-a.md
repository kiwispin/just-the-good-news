Scientists are developing eco-friendly coatings inspired by the natural structure of butterfly wings, offering a safer alternative to traditional chemical-based pigments. This innovative approach could drastically reduce toxic waste in the paint industry while providing brighter, longer-lasting colors. By mimicking nature, researchers are opening the door to sustainable, non-toxic materials for everyday use.
