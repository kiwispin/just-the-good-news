MIT neuroscientists have discovered that the brain's capacity for logical reasoning operates independently from its language-processing systems. The study found that individuals with severe language impairments from strokes could solve complex logic puzzles just as effectively as others, opening encouraging new perspectives on human cognitive resilience.
