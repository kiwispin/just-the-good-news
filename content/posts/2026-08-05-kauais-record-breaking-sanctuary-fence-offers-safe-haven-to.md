A massive 11,200-foot predator exclusion fence on the Hawaiian island of Kauai is successfully safeguarding 168 acres of critical nesting habitat. By keeping out feral predators, the barrier allows thousands of vulnerable ground-nesting seabirds to safely raise their young and rebuild their populations.
