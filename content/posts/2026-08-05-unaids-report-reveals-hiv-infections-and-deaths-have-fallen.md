A landmark report from UNAIDS shows that global HIV infections and AIDS-related deaths have dropped to their lowest levels in over three decades. Furthermore, seven diverse countries—including Kenya, Nepal, and Rwanda—have reached major milestones in treatment access and prevention, signaling historic progress in the global fight against the epidemic.
