A promising new study shows that the active ingredient in treatments like Mounjaro and Zepbound can activate brown fat, a beneficial type of tissue that naturally burns calories to generate heat. This discovery suggests the treatments do more than just suppress appetite, potentially offering a more holistic approach to metabolic health and diabetes management.
