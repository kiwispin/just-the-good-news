Common B-Vitamin Derivative Shows Promise in Treating Psoriasis Symptoms

Good News Network 31 October 2026

Photo: Lucas Vasques / Unsplash

Recent research reveals that a derivative of vitamin B could offer a simple, accessible treatment for psoriasis, an autoimmune condition that causes itchy, scaly skin patches. Studies conducted in both human and animal trials demonstrated encouraging results in significantly reducing symptoms.
