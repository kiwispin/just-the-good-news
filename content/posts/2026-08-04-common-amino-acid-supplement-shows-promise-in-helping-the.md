Researchers have discovered that the dietary supplement arginine helps the immune system detect threats by boosting a crucial cellular warning protein. In laboratory studies, arginine-rich diets successfully led to fewer colon tumors and milder viral infections, offering a promising, accessible pathway for future therapies.
