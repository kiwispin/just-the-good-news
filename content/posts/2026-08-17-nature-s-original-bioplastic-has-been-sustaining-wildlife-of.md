Researchers have discovered that dozens of animal species possess natural enzymes capable of breaking down and digesting microbial bioplastics. This groundbreaking finding reveals that nature's own biodegradable materials have been safely integrated into the global food web for hundreds of millions of years, offering inspiring new insights for modern green technology.
