Over the past 15 years, three wind turbines on Ross Island have successfully overcome harsh polar conditions to power McMurdo Station, significantly reducing the continent's reliance on fossil fuels. This pioneering wind farm has kept 122,000 gallons of diesel from being burned, proving that renewable energy can thrive in the world's most extreme environments.
