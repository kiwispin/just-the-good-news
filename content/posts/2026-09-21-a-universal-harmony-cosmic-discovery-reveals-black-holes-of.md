Astronomers have discovered a surprising universal rule connecting black holes, finding that both supermassive giants and smaller stellar-mass black holes fire powerful cosmic jets at the exact same feeding threshold. This breakthrough simplifies our understanding of the universe, proving that these mysterious cosmic giants are far more predictable and unified in their behavior than previously thought.
