Helga Duffy has officially been recognized by Guinness World Records as the world's oldest wing-walker after taking to the skies strapped atop a small plane at Rendcomb Airfield. Encouraged by staff at her assisted-living facility, Duffy embraced the high-flying adventure to achieve the remarkable milestone.
