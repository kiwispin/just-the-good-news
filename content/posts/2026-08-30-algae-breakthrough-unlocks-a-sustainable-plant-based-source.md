Scientists have successfully grown spirulina with high levels of active vitamin B12, matching the nutrient density of beef by optimizing light conditions during cultivation. This carbon-neutral innovation offers a highly sustainable, accessible alternative for those seeking plant-based sources of the essential vitamin.
