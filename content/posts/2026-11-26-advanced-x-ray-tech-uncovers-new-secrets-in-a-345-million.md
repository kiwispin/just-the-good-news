Using modern X-ray imaging, scientists discovered hidden gills and thousands of tiny teeth in a famous prehistoric fossil, revealing it to be an aquatic creature rather than an early reptile. The finding offers exciting new insights into prehistoric evolution, showing that key traits for life on land began much earlier in water-dependent animals.
