By studying museum specimens dating back to the 1800s, scientists have officially recognized a distinct new frog species, Pristimantis milpe, living in Ecuador and Colombia. This discovery, after more than a century of mistaken identity, highlights the incredible biodiversity still waiting to be understood and protected in our world's ecosystems.
