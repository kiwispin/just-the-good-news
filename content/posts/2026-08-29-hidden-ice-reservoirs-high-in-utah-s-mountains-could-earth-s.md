Using advanced gravity-mapping technology, scientists have discovered a massive, ancient reservoir of pure ice safely preserved beneath a rocky slope in Utah. This breakthrough reveals that similar rock glaciers worldwide likely store tens of gigatons of hidden water, offering a resilient and naturally protected water resource for our warming planet.
