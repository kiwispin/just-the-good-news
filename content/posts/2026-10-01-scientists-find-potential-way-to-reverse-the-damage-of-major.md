Researchers have discovered that the harmful effects of the APOE4 gene, which damages brain blood vessels and hinders waste removal, can be reversed in laboratory experiments. This breakthrough reveals promising new targets for treating and potentially reversing the progression of Alzheimer’s and other neurodegenerative diseases.
