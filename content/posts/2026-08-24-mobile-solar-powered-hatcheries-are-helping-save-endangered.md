To combat declining populations, conservationists have launched innovative mobile hatcheries designed to shelter queen conch snails during their most vulnerable early life stages. Powered by solar energy, these roaming labs provide a safe harbor to help more of the marine mollusks reach reproductive age and restore vital ocean ecosystems.
