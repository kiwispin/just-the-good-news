New research suggests that the mental effort involved in identifying birds helps preserve neuroplasticity, a crucial aspect of cognitive health that typically diminishes as we age. By challenging the brain to recognize subtle species details, birdwatchers can actively build resilience against cognitive decline. Even casual birders may benefit from the mentally engaging nature of this outdoor hobby.
