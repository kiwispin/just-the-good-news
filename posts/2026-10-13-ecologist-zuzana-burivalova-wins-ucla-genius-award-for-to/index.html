Ecologist Zuzana Burivalova Wins UCLA Genius Award for Listening to Forest Health

Mongabay 13 October 2026

Photo: Olah Zsolt Istvan / Unsplash

Tropical ecologist Zuzana Burivalova has been awarded the prestigious 2026 Pritzker Emerging Environmental Genius Award by UCLA. Her innovative work uses bioacoustics—listening to the sounds of nature—to monitor and protect forest ecosystems worldwide.
