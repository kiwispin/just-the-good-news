Erik's Ranch & Retreats in Montana is redefining hospitality by employing autistic individuals as tour guides, wranglers, and concierges across its 230-acre property. The social enterprise offers guests memorable wilderness experiences while providing staff members with fulfilling, long-term employment and personal independence. Through hands-on roles tailored to their talents, team members build valuable skills in an supportive community setting.
