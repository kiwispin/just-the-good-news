Scientists at the Large Hadron Collider have discovered that even small particle collisions can recreate quark-gluon plasma, the fundamental matter present just after the Big Bang. This surprising finding offers researchers an accessible new way to study the fundamental building blocks of the cosmos and better understand how our universe formed.
