Researchers have discovered that ant caregiving is driven by the same chemical brain signals that regulate hunger, with these patterns shifting as the insects age. Because similar pathways govern mammalian nurturing, the findings suggest that nature uses a universal, elegant blueprint to foster love and care across wildly different species.
