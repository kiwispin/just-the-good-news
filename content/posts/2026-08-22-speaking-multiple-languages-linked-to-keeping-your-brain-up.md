A new study reveals that multilingualism may significantly slow down brain aging, with those speaking four or more languages showing brains that appear over a decade younger than monolinguals. The cognitive benefits are especially pronounced for those who start learning early and achieve high fluency, offering a promising, lifelong boost to brain health.
