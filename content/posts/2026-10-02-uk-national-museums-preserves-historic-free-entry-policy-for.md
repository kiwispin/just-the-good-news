Following collaborative discussions between government and tourism leaders, the UK’s 14 national museums—including the British Museum and the Natural History Museum—will continue to offer free admission to visitors from around the globe. This decision safeguards a 25-year tradition of keeping world-class cultural heritage open and accessible to all.
