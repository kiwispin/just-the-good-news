A non-invasive, genetic urine test has shown exceptional accuracy in a recent UK observational study, successfully identifying cancer-associated genes in the vast majority of patients who later developed a malignancy. This state-of-the-art screening tool offers a highly reliable, pain-free method for ruling out bladder cancer early.
