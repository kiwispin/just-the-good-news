Researchers have identified rare flavoalkaloids and dozens of previously unreported phenolic compounds in cannabis leaves, which are typically discarded as agricultural waste. This discovery reveals that these overlooked leaves are a rich source of bioactive molecules with significant potential for new medical treatments.
