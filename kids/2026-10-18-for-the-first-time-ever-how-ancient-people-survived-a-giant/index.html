For the First Time Ever: How Ancient People Survived a Giant Volcano!

Sunday 18 October 2026 •ScienceDaily

Photo: Cosmic Timetraveler / Unsplash

7,300 years ago, a massive volcano exploded in Japan and covered the land in hot ash, but amazing new clues show that ancient people didn't give up! Instead of disappearing, clever families packed up their things, moved to safer places, and actually built even better villages together. Isn't it cool to think about how brave and creative humans can be when big troubles come their way?
