Using advanced LiDAR technology and citizen-science teamwork, researchers have mapped a spectacular, carbon-rich forest in Taiwan's remote mountains home to hundreds of giant Taiwania firs, including a record-breaking 84.1-meter tree. This newly mapped 'temple of giants' represents one of the most carbon-dense forest ecosystems on the planet, offering a hopeful blueprint for conservation.
