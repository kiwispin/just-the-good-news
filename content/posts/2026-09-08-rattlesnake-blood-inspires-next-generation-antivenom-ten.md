Researchers have discovered that natural proteins in rattlesnake blood can be combined to highly neutralize venom from multiple dangerous snake species. In laboratory tests, these nature-inspired mixtures proved roughly ten times more potent than existing commercial options, paving the way for safer and more effective snakebite therapies.
