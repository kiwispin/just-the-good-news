Liverpool Tour Bus Gets the Ultimate Surprise as Paul McCartney Pulls Up Beside Them

Good News Network 25 July 2026

Photo: Alexander David / Unsplash

Beatles fans aboard Liverpool's Magical Mystery Tour bus experienced a once-in-a-lifetime moment when Paul McCartney pulled up right next to them in his car. Making the surprise even more unforgettable, the music legend was actively on a video call with his former bandmate, Ringo Starr, at the very moment he waved to the delighted crowd.
