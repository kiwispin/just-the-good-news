Researchers at Edith Cowan University have discovered that magnetite, a mineral abundant in Western Australia's Pilbara region, could play a vital role in generating clean energy. This breakthrough offers a promising foundation for a major green hydrogen export industry, transforming the country's vast red deserts into a powerhouse for sustainable technology.
