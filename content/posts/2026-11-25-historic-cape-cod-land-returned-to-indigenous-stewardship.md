The Nature Conservancy has transferred 126 acres of coastal salt marsh and natural habitat in Eastham, Massachusetts, to the Native Land Conservancy. This Indigenous-led nonprofit will now protect and manage the ecologically vital land, restoring native ownership to the region four centuries after European settlement.
