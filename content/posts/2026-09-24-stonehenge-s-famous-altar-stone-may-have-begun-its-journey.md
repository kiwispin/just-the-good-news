A new theory suggests Stonehenge's six-ton Altar Stone was carried from Scotland by glaciers before being thoughtfully transported by prehistoric people to Salisbury Plain. This remarkable multistage journey reveals a deep, long-standing connection to the stone, suggesting it was treasured by ancient communities long before the monument was completed.
