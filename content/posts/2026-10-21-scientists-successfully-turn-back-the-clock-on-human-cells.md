Researchers have successfully reprogrammed blood cells from an 80-year-old donor into neural stem cells with a cellular age under 20. This laboratory breakthrough provides a valuable new tool for studying cellular rejuvenation and could advance our understanding of age-related conditions like Alzheimer's.
