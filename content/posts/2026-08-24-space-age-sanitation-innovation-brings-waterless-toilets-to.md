Originally conceived for NASA astronauts, the waterless 'iThrone' toilet is now providing safe, dignified sanitation to over 20,000 households across Uganda, Panama, and the United States. This innovative technology evaporates human waste without plumbing, offering a vital solution for communities facing severe water scarcity and lack of infrastructure.
