Scientists have discovered that silver nanocatalysts possess a natural 'switch' that automatically shifts where reactions occur depending on the energy task at hand. This breakthrough could lead to smarter, highly efficient catalyst designs that simultaneously boost renewable power generation and lower the energy needed to produce green hydrogen.
