By stretching a microscopic film of ruthenium dioxide to just a few atoms thick, researchers have successfully activated 'altermagnetism' in a material previously thought to be nonmagnetic. This breakthrough offers a promising new pathway for developing faster, more energy-efficient computer chips and advanced electronics.
