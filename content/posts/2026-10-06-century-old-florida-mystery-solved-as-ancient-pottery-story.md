Archaeologists have discovered that ancient pottery found in Tampa Bay was crafted by skilled migrant artisans who traveled hundreds of miles, keeping their heritage alive. By blending crushed fragments of their original homeland ceramics into new vessels, these craftspeople beautifully preserved a physical link to their ancestry across generations.
