Astronomers have cracked a cosmic puzzle by discovering that mysterious, compact red dots in the early universe are actually the bright cores of fully formed spiral galaxies. By studying a nearby galaxy nicknamed the 'Saguaro,' researchers proved that these distant red dots are not isolated anomalies, but the vibrant hearts of vast stellar systems previously hidden by distance.
