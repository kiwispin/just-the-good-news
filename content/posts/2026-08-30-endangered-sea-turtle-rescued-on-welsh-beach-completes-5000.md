After a two-year rescue and recovery journey that began when he washed ashore near death in Wales, a rare Kemp’s ridley sea turtle named Rhossi has successfully flown back to the Gulf of Mexico. Thanks to an international team of caretakers, the endangered turtle is settling into a Texas rehabilitation facility in preparation for his final release back into the wild.
