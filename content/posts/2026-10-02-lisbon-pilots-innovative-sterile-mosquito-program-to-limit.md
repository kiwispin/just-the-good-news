Portugal has successfully completed the initial phase of a eco-friendly initiative in Lisbon, releasing 30,000 sterile male mosquitoes to naturally curb the population of invasive disease-carrying species. This targeted, chemical-free biopesticide method offers a promising and safe way to protect communities from tropical diseases like dengue and Zika.
