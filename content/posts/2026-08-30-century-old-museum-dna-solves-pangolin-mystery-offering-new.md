By analyzing DNA from a 190-year-old museum specimen, scientists have identified a distinct Himalayan pangolin species living in Nepal and northern India. This genetic breakthrough provides conservationists with a powerful new tool to trace confiscated scales back to their exact origins and better protect these vulnerable animals.
