New research reveals that low-dose aspirin may offer significant brain protection for older adults with a specific genetic marker, cutting their relative risk of dementia by 70%. The discovery opens the door for more personalized approaches to preventative cognitive health, helping doctors identify who can safely benefit from the accessible medication.
