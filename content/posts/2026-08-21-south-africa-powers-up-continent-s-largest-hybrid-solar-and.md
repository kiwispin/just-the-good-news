A major new hybrid renewable energy facility in South Africa's Northern Cape has officially launched, combining a 216-megawatt solar plant with advanced battery storage. This landmark project is set to generate 400 gigawatt-hours of clean electricity annually, providing reliable power to approximately 200,000 homes.
