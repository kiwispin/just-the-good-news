Dolly Parton’s Book-Gifting Program Proven to Boost Early Literacy and Love of Reading Worldwide

Good Good Good 26 August 2026

Photo: Mr. Great Heart / Unsplash

A comprehensive review of global data confirms that children enrolled in Dolly Parton’s Imagination Library show significantly stronger early literacy skills and a greater enthusiasm for reading. By delivering free books directly to homes, the initiative is successfully laying a vital foundation for lifelong learning.
