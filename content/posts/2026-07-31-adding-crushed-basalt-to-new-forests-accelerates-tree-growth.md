Scientists from Imperial College London have discovered that spreading crushed rock on newly planted forests significantly boosts soil nutrients and speeds up tree development. This natural 'enhanced weathering' technique represents a highly promising and scalable breakthrough for maximizing the carbon-absorbing power of young woodlands.
