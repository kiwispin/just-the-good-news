Enchanting Hidden Fluorescence Discovered in European Fire Salamanders

ScienceDaily 29 October 2026

Photo: Wolfgang Hasselmann / Unsplash

Researchers have discovered that Europe's black-and-yellow fire salamanders naturally fluoresce with vibrant green and cyan hues under ultraviolet light. The gentle glow originates from skin glands and secretions, revealing a fascinating new dimension to a well-known species.
