Scientists have identified a 150-million-year-old fossil found in Spain as the first confirmed Diplodocus outside North America. This remarkable find suggests these gentle giants traveled across prehistoric land bridges, offering researchers exciting new insights into how Jurassic dinosaurs connected across continents.
