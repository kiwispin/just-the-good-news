Using its own independent observations, Canada's CHIME radio telescope has successfully captured the faint, nine-billion-year-old glow of hydrogen. This milestone confirms a long-theorized method for mapping the distant universe, providing scientists with a powerful new tool to measure cosmic expansion and study dark energy.
