A study conducted off the coast of Cornwall, England, shows that simple visual deterrents can dramatically reduce accidental seabird catches. Nets featuring bird-of-prey kites lowered the odds of trapping guillemots and razorbills by 71%, while spinning 'looming-eye' buoys reduced incidents by 59%.
