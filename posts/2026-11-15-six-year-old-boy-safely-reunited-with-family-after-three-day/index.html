Six-Year-Old Boy Safely Reunited with Family After Three-Day Search in Kentucky

Good News Network 15 November 2026

Photo: Mona Rivas / Unsplash

A six-year-old boy with autism who went missing for three days has been found safe after walking onto a horse trail in Kentucky. Local volunteers and rescue groups worked tirelessly throughout the multi-day effort, which ended happily when a trail rider spotted the young boy.
