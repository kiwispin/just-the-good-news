Researchers have identified a key genetic link to spinal degeneration and discovered that a widely available osteoporosis drug can prevent harmful mineral buildup in spinal tissues. This promising finding could lead to preventative treatments for a major source of chronic back pain, offering hope for long-term mobility and relief.
