By analyzing over 3,000 fossilized bones from Wyoming, researchers have identified rare 66-million-year-old tooth traces that match the unique bite patterns of a Tyrannosaurus rex. This discovery provides paleontologists with valuable new insights into the ancient predator's actual feeding behavior.
