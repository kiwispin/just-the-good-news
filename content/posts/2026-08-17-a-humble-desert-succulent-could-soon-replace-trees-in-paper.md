American consumer goods giant Kimberly-Clark is exploring the use of hesperaloe, a drought-resistant desert plant, as a sustainable alternative to wood fiber for everyday essentials like tissues and paper towels. This innovative shift could significantly reduce the paper industry's reliance on forestry, offering a highly efficient and eco-friendly raw material that thrives in arid climates.
