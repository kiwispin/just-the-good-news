After nearly freezing to death during a mass cold-stunning event off Cape Cod last year, a rare Kemp's ridley sea turtle named Pancake has been successfully rehabilitated and released back into the wild. Pancake was one of hundreds of turtles saved by dedicated rescue teams who nursed the endangered animals back to health.
