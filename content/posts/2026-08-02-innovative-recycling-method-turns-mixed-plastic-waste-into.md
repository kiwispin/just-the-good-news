Scientists have developed a low-temperature chemical process that converts three common types of unsorted plastic waste into high-purity hydrogen. By trapping the carbon in solid or liquid forms rather than releasing it as greenhouse gases, this breakthrough offers a double win for green energy and waste reduction.
