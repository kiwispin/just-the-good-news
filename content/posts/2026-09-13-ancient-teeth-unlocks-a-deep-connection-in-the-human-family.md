Scientists studying 400,000-year-old fossilized teeth have discovered a shared protein mutation linking ancient Homo erectus populations to Denisovans and modern people in Southeast Asia and Oceania. This fascinating find maps a continuous, resilient thread of human connection stretching back hundreds of thousands of years.
