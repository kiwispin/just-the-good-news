A breathtaking new infrared image from the James Webb Space Telescope has captured NGC 2392, beautifully illustrating how a dying Sun-like star can create stellar art. The cosmic portrait reveals a glowing white dwarf illuminating a majestic 'mane' of dust and gas, offering scientists a spectacular and detailed look at the lifecycle of stars.
