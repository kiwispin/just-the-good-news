Scientists have developed a specially engineered chewing gum that successfully targets and reduces HPV levels in the mouth by up to 93 percent. Crucially, the treatment also eliminates harmful, cancer-linked bacteria while preserving the mouth's beneficial microbiome, offering hope for an accessible and affordable preventative therapy.
