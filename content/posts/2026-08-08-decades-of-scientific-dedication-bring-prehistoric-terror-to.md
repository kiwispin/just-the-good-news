After more than 40 years of dedicated research, paleontologists have created the world's first scientifically accurate, life-sized replica of the massive Deinosuchus schwimmeri. Now on display at Georgia's Tellus Science Museum, the impressive 31-foot skeleton offers visitors an unprecedented look into our planet's ancient history.
