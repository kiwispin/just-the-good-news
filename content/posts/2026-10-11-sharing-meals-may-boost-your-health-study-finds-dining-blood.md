Researchers at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem have discovered that eating a meal with a close companion leads to significantly better blood sugar regulation than eating the exact same food alone. The findings highlight the powerful physiological benefits of human connection and sharing everyday experiences. This research suggests that gathering around the dinner table offers a simple, natural way to support metabolic health.
