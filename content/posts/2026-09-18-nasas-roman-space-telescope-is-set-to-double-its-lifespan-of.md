Thanks to an incredibly precise course correction and extra fuel efficiency, NASA's upcoming Roman Space Telescope is now expected to operate for at least 22 years, more than twice its original design life. This surplus of fuel means the powerful observatory will be able to study the mysteries of the universe for decades longer than planned, maximizing its scientific potential.
