Scientists have solved a long-standing space mystery, discovering that young Jupiter's powerful magnetic field acted as a protective shield that allowed its multiple giant moons to safely form and survive. This exciting breakthrough offers astronomers a clearer understanding of how planetary systems develop both in our solar system and across the wider universe.
