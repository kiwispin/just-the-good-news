By analyzing mutations in bacteria and yeast, researchers have updated a 50-year-old evolutionary theory, revealing that essential genes mutate at the same rate as others. This discovery clears up long-standing misconceptions in genetics, helping scientists better understand the true mechanisms of natural selection.
