Scientists have discovered that electrons can form the stable pairs needed for zero-resistance electricity at much higher temperatures than previously thought possible. By confirming a crucial piece of the puzzle can exist at room temperature, researchers now have a clearer roadmap toward unlocking revolutionary energy efficiency. This key insight brings humanity one step closer to practical, room-temperature power grids and advanced technology.
