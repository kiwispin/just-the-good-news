By blocking a specific protein that increases with age, scientists have successfully prompted both animal and human joint tissue to naturally regenerate healthy, functional cartilage. This breakthrough could pave the way for non-invasive treatments that prevent osteoarthritis and potentially eliminate the need for joint replacement surgeries.
