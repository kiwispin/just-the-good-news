By repurposing orbital data from 1,200 Starlink satellites, researchers have successfully mapped a notoriously hard-to-reach layer of Earth's upper atmosphere. This innovative scanning method will significantly improve satellite tracking and help prevent collisions in our increasingly crowded orbit.
