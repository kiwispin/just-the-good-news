Observations of the interstellar comet 3I/ATLAS have revealed unprecedentedly high levels of methanol, offering astronomers an incredibly detailed look at how chemistry behaves in other star systems. The discovery, made using the ALMA telescope, also showed tiny icy grains acting like miniature comets, shedding light on the diverse conditions that shape our universe.
