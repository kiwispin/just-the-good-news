New computer simulations reveal that powerful magnetic fields can help white dwarf stars expand up to 2.4 times the mass of our Sun, far surpassing a long-standing limit in astrophysics. This breakthrough offers scientists a clear explanation for extraordinarily bright supernovae, solving a decades-old cosmic mystery.
