Scientists have discovered that tiny amounts of airborne sodium cause hydrogen flames to shift from bright orange to nearly invisible. This breakthrough paves the way for specialized sensors in future hydrogen appliances, ensuring clean-energy stoves and ovens can be used safely in everyday kitchens.
