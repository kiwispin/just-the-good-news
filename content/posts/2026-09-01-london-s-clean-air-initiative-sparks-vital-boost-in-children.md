Seven years after the introduction of London's Ultra Low Emission Zone (Ulez), researchers have found a direct link between the reduced air pollution and healthier lung development in local children. This landmark study offers encouraging evidence of how targeted urban environmental policies can create immediate, tangible benefits for the next generation's well-being.
