Retired teacher Rob Burke is repurposing discarded musical instruments into charming outdoor feeding stations for local wildlife in Prince Edward Island. Dubbed the Frets and Feathers Cafe, his creative upcycling project offers backyard birds a place to dine while keeping old instruments out of landfills.
