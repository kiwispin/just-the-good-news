On August 12, 2026, a spectacular total solar eclipse will sweep across Europe, offering a rare look at the Sun's hidden outer atmosphere. To ensure everyone can experience this awe-inspiring event, the European Space Agency will broadcast a live high-definition stream from Spain's Javalambre Observatory, complete with drone footage and expert insights.
