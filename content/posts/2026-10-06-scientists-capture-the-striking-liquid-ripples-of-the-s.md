Physicists at CERN have observed the clearest evidence yet that the primordial soup of the early universe behaves like a perfect liquid, watching quarks leave ripples and wakes as they travel. This breakthrough discovery provides researchers with a clearer understanding of how our newborn universe first began to flow and form.
