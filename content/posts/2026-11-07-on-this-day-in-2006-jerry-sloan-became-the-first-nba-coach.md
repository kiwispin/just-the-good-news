Seventeen years ago today, Utah Jazz head coach Jerry Sloan reached a landmark milestone by becoming the first coach in professional basketball history to achieve 1,000 victories with a single franchise. Celebrated for his loyalty and resilience, Sloan spent nearly his entire 23-year coaching career guiding the Jazz through every era of the team's history. His achievement remains a testament to long-term dedication and excellence in professional sports.
