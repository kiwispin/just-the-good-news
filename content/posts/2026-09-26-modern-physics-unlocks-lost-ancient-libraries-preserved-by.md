In an exciting breakthrough for historical preservation, scientists have discovered that trace amounts of lead in ancient ink allow X-ray scans to read carbonized papyrus scrolls without unrolling them. This non-destructive technique opens the door to translating hundreds of long-lost Greek and Roman texts that were previously thought to be unreadable. This innovative intersection of science and history promises to reshape our understanding of classical literature.
