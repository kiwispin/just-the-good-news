Researchers at Stanford University have used artificial intelligence to identify a natural molecule that significantly reduces appetite and encourages fat loss in animal studies. Known as BRP, the compound achieves these results without the common digestive side effects or muscle loss associated with current treatments, opening the door for more precise future health therapies.
