Archaeologists in Peru have discovered an entirely intact, 600-year-old tomb within the UNESCO World Heritage site of Chan Chan, preserving invaluable history from the Chimu empire. This rare, unlooted site contains 38 skeletons and a wealth of artifacts, offering researchers an unprecedented look into the lives and culture of the region's ancestors.
