In a historic first, a female Amur tiger has been successfully released into Kazakhstan's Ile-Balkhash Nature Reserve as part of a decade-long ecosystem restoration project. This milestone follows years of dedicated reforestation and the replenishment of native prey species to ensure the tigers can thrive in their ancestral habitat.
