Researchers in the Netherlands have discovered that a rare type of immature brain cell persists in adults over 80, behaving differently in those who maintain their cognitive health despite showing physical signs of Alzheimer's. This discovery offers valuable new insights into how the human brain naturally resists dementia, opening potential pathways for protective therapies.
