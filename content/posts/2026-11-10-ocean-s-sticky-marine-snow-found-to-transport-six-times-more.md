Scientists have discovered that naturally sticky underwater particles resist bacterial breakdown, allowing them to sink deeper into the ocean than previously realized. This sticky 'marine snow' captures up to six times more carbon, revealing a powerful, natural mechanism helping to regulate Earth's climate.
