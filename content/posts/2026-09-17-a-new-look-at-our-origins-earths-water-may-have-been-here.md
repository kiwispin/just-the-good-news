A groundbreaking study suggests Earth formed almost entirely from inner Solar System materials, indicating that our planet's life-giving water was likely present much closer to the young Sun than previously believed. This fascinating discovery challenges long-held theories and offers scientists a simpler, more local explanation for how our vibrant blue planet came to be.
