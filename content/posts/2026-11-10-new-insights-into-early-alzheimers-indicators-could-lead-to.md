Scientists studying human cell models have discovered that genetic markers for inherited Alzheimer's can affect nerve-to-muscle connections independently of the brain. This key finding helps explain early changes in physical balance and coordination, opening potential new avenues for identifying and supporting patients well before memory symptoms appear.
