A Walk Through Time: First Adult T. Rex Trackway Discovered in North Dakota

Good News Network 12 September 2026

Photo: Arek Adeoye / Unsplash

Paleontologists have confirmed the historic discovery of the first-ever sequence of footprints left by an adult Tyrannosaurus rex. This 66-million-year-old trackway offers scientists an unprecedented look into how the iconic dinosaur walked and moved across the ancient landscape.
