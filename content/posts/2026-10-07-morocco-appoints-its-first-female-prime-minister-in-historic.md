King Mohammed VI has appointed Fatima Ezzahra El Mansouri, a 50-year-old urban planner and the longtime mayor of Marrakesh, as Morocco's new prime minister. This landmark appointment marks the first time a woman has held the nation's highest ministerial office, representing a major step forward for leadership representation in the region.
