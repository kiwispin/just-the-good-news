Scientists studying a 66-million-year-old fossilized feather have discovered that the ancestors of modern birds likely survived the historic asteroid impact thanks to their superior, highly insulating plumage. This ancient discovery helps solve a long-standing evolutionary mystery, highlighting the incredible resilience and adaptation of the avian species we share the planet with today.
