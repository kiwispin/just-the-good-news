In a historic vote, the United Nations has chosen to phase out the traditional 450-year-old Mercator map projection in favor of a model that accurately represents the true scale of the continents. This change ensures that regions like Africa, which is actually fourteen times larger than Greenland, are finally depicted in their true, majestic proportions. This landmark decision marks a significant step forward for global education and visual equality.
