Scientists at Caltech have engineered tiny, balloon-shaped nanoscale vehicles capable of safely crossing the brain's protective barriers to deliver targeted treatments for cancer and neurological disorders. Once their therapeutic payload is delivered, the microscopic carriers simply biodegrade without leaving behind harmful residue.
