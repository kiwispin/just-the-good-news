A UK conservation charity has successfully restored an industrial wasteland once used for canal dredging waste into a vital wetland habitat. The thriving sanctuary now hosts 64% of the UK's nesting population of rare black-necked grebes, showcasing how industrial sites can be successfully reclaimed for biodiversity.
