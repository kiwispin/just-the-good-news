Scientists have officially recognized a new, nonvenomous reddish-brown groundsnake species, naming it Lielaphis slashi after the Guns N’ Roses guitarist and lifelong reptile enthusiast. Discovered through genetic testing and physical analysis, this unique lizard-eating snake marks an exciting addition to the region's rich biodiversity.
