A student team from National Taiwan Normal University has developed 'Reefvive,' an innovative artificial reef made from recycled oyster shells and coral gravel. Unlike smooth traditional concrete, this textured, natural material provides the perfect surface for young coral to attach and grow, turning aquaculture waste into a lifeline for marine ecosystems.
