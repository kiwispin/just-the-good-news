A Texas family has sold 650 acres of pristine land along Lake Palestine to ensure its natural beauty is protected for future generations. The expansive property, featuring pine forests, wetlands, and a pecan orchard, represents the lake's longest remaining undeveloped shoreline and will soon be open to the public as a state park.
