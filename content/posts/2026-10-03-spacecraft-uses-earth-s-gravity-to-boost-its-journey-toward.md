The European Space Agency's Juice spacecraft successfully executed a gravity-assist flyby of Earth, saving precious fuel while gaining a significant speed boost toward Jupiter. This precision maneuver also allowed scientists to successfully test the spacecraft's advanced instruments on both the Earth and the Moon, ensuring it is in peak condition for its deep-space mission.
