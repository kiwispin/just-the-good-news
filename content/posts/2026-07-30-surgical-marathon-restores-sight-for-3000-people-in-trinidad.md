Over a remarkable 12-day period, a community hospital in Trinidad and Tobago successfully cleared a massive backlog of eye surgeries, restoring vision to thousands of patients. This life-changing initiative was made possible through the collaboration of US and Caribbean Seventh-day Adventist churches, who organized and funded the cataract and pterygium procedures entirely free of cost.
