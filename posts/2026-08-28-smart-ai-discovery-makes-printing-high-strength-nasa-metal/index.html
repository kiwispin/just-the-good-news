Smart AI Discovery Makes Printing High-Strength NASA Metal Much More Affordable

ScienceDaily 28 August 2026

Photo: D koi / Unsplash

Using advanced AI to analyze over 100 million printing combinations, researchers discovered a way to 3D-print a high-performance NASA alloy using a record-low 500 watts of power. This breakthrough means the durable material, previously expensive and difficult to manufacture, can now be produced using standard, widely available 3D printers.
