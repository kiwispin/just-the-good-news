By analyzing over 900 embryos with advanced CT scans, researchers have discovered that snake embryos naturally coil into gentle spirals because their bodies grow faster than their internal systems. This fascinating biological breakthrough provides a clearer understanding of how complex animal shapes form and develop in nature.
