Every year on October 3, both North and South Korea celebrate Gaecheonjeol, or National Foundation Day, honoring the legendary descent of Hwanung 4,483 years ago. This unique holiday serves as a meaningful cultural bridge, uniting the entire peninsula in celebration of their shared mythical origins and heritage.
