In India’s Tiger Reserves, Women Guides Are Breaking Barriers and Leading the Way

Reasons to be Cheerful 27 August 2026

Photo: Lakshmi Narasimha / Unsplash

At the Tadoba-Andhari Tiger Reserve in Maharashtra, local women are stepping into the traditionally male-dominated role of safari guides. By mastering wildlife tracking and guiding visitors, they are earning financial independence while challenging gender stereotypes in conservation tourism.
