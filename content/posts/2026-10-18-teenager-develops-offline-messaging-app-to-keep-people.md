Inspired by the loss of communications following major earthquakes in Türkiye and Syria, a high school student created a disaster messaging app that operates without cellular networks or internet. The software leverages Bluetooth technology to 'hop' messages between nearby devices, providing a vital communication lifeline when standard infrastructure fails.
