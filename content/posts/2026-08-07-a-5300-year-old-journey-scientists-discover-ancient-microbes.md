By successfully separating ancient microorganisms from modern ones on the famous 5,300-year-old glacier mummy, researchers have mapped Ötzi's original gut bacteria and discovered cold-loving yeasts that survived for millennia. This scientific breakthrough offers researchers a clearer window into the history of human health and the resilient nature of microscopic life.
