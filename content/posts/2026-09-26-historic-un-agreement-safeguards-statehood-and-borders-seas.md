In a landmark decision, the United Nations has adopted its first-ever declaration ensuring that rising sea levels will not alter recognized national borders. This historic agreement guarantees that low-lying island nations retain their official sovereignty, maritime zones, and international rights even if their physical land is impacted by climate change.
