Scientists have found that an experimental compound called TOFA can boost energy expenditure and reduce body fat in mice without causing significant muscle loss. When paired with existing GLP-1 medications, the treatment also showed improved results for blood sugar and liver health, offering a promising new avenue for metabolic care.
