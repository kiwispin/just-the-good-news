A dedicated group of teenage environmentalists in California has officially set a Guinness World Record by collecting and recycling approximately 22,000 tennis and pickleballs in a single day. Their efforts successfully diverted 2,675 pounds of rubber and plastic waste from local landfills, turning discarded sports gear into a win for the planet.
