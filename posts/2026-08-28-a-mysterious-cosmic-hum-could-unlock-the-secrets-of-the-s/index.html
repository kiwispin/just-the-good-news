A Mysterious Cosmic Hum Could Unlock the Secrets of the Universe's First Stars and Dark Matter

ScienceDaily 28 August 2026

Photo: Jeremy Thomas / Unsplash

Scientists have discovered that a gentle background hum of gravitational waves may be the legacy of hypothetical 'Dark Stars' from the dawn of time. This exciting finding offers astronomers a groundbreaking new pathway to study how the universe's earliest supermassive black holes formed and to finally unravel the mysteries of dark matter.
