A conservation program in Bangladesh's Sundarbans has seen surprising success after released saltwater crocodiles chose to settle into their new natural habitat instead of trying to return to their breeding facility. This behavior suggests that releasing larger, mature captive crocodiles is a highly effective strategy for restoring depleted wild populations.
