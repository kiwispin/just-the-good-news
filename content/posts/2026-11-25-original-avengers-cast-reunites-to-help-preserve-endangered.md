Stars from Marvel's original Avengers cast have joined forces for a real-world mission to safeguard a nearly endangered Native American language. By leveraging their global platform, the actors are helping bring vital attention and resources to Indigenous language revitalization efforts. This collaborative initiative aims to introduce the rich linguistic heritage to a worldwide audience and support cultural preservation.
