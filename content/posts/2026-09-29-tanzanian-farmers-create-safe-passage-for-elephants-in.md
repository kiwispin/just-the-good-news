In Tanzania, 368 local farmers generously set aside portions of their land to establish a crucial nine-mile wildlife corridor connecting the savanna to the Udzungwa Mountains. Recent trail camera footage confirms the initiative's success, showing that 529 elephants have already safely used the path to navigate the region.
