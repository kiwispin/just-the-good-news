A new study reveals that the historical embrace of the potato as a staple crop has shaped the DNA of Indigenous Peruvians, leaving them with an exceptionally high number of starch-digestion genes. This discovery offers powerful evidence of how ancestral diets can positively influence human evolution and genetic adaptation over thousands of years.
