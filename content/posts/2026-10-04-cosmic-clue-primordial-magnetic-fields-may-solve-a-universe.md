Scientists have discovered that tiny magnetic fields formed just after the Big Bang may finally explain the discrepancy in how fast our universe is expanding. Detailed simulations show these ancient fields influenced early hydrogen formation, providing researchers with a major breakthrough in understanding the history of our cosmos.
