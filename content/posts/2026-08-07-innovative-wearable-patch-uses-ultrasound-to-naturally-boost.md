A groundbreaking wearable device has been shown to help people reach restorative REM sleep 43 minutes faster and extend its duration without the need for medication. The non-invasive ultrasound patch also demonstrated promising signs of improving stress regulation and emotional brain chemistry during its initial 28-participant study.
