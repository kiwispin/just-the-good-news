Wildlife observers are celebrating after olive ridley sea turtles were documented nesting on the West Coast of the United States for the very first time. Marine biologists captured rare daylight footage of a female successfully laying her eggs on a Southern California beach, marking an exciting potential expansion for the species' habitat.
