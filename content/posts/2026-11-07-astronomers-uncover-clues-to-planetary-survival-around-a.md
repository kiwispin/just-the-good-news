Scientists studying a white dwarf star have detected two distinct rings of cosmic debris with a gap that likely signals the survival of smaller planetary bodies. The discovery offers fascinating new insights into how celestial objects can endure the life cycle of their host stars, shedding light on the long-term future of systems like our own.
