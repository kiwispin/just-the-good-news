Scientists have discovered that a specific protein found in bullfrogs could serve as the first effective antidote to saxitoxin, the potent neurotoxin responsible for paralytic shellfish poisoning. This breakthrough offers a potential cure for a severe, foodborne illness that previously had no specific treatment beyond supportive care. By mimicking how bullfrogs naturally neutralize the toxin, researchers hope to develop life-saving therapies for humans.
