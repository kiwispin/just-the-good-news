For the fourth consecutive year, Indian grey hornbills have successfully bred in India's Gujarat state, marking a major comeback after being absent from the region for over half a century. This ecological milestone highlights the vital importance of preserving mature, old-growth trees, which provide the essential nesting habitats these unique birds need to thrive.
