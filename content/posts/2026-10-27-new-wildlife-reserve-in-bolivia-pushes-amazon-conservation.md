Rainforest Trust has assisted in establishing a 715,000-acre wildlife reserve along Bolivia's Iténez River, protecting a critical corridor for biodiversity including jaguars and Amazon River dolphins. With this milestone, the organization has now helped safeguard over 70 million acres across the Amazon basin.
