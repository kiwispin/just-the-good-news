By subjecting diamonds to pressures greater than those found inside Neptune, scientists have resolved a 20-year-old physics mystery regarding how carbon behaves under extreme stress. This breakthrough not only sheds light on the exotic 'diamond rain' of ice giant planets but also offers key insights that could help optimize clean fusion energy production here on Earth.
