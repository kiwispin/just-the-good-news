Zion National Park has become the first in the US national park system to offer a specialized all-terrain wheelchair for public use, allowing visitors with mobility challenges to explore the stunning canyon trails. This milestone was made possible by a charitable nonprofit that funded and donated the rugged equipment to ensure the park's natural beauty is accessible to everyone.
