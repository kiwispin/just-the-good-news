Fat Bear Week Crowns Its 2024 Champion with a Delightful Digital Celebration

Good Good Good 30 September 2026

Photo: Mark Basarab / Unsplash

The beloved annual Fat Bear Week competition has successfully concluded, celebrating the remarkable pre-hibernation weight gain of Alaska's brown bears. To honor the occasion, Google joined the festivities by launching a playful, interactive animation that brought extra joy to fans tracking the bears' progress online.
