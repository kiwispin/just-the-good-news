To help boost local recycling efforts, household brand Lam Soon is introducing over 100 automated refill stations across Singapore for its Bio-Home cleaning line. By eliminating the cost of single-use packaging, the initiative allows shoppers to bring their own bottles and top up on laundry and home cleaners at a 30% discount.
