A study from UTHealth Houston involving nearly 200,000 older adults found that receiving a high-dose flu shot is associated with a nearly 55% lower risk of developing Alzheimer's disease. This builds on previous research, showing that enhanced immune support from standard vaccines may offer even greater protection for long-term brain health.
