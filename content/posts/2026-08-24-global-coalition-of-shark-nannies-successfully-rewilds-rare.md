An international consortium of 60 aquariums has successfully released 82 captive-bred zebra shark pups into the wild waters of Indonesia, where the species was previously pushed to the brink of extinction. This groundbreaking initiative marks the first-ever successful rewilding of zebra sharks, offering a bright future for the region's marine biodiversity.
