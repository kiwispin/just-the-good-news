Scientists analyzing a meteorite that recently landed in New Jersey have discovered ancient organic compounds, amino acids, and evidence of salty fluids. Because the sample was recovered immediately, its pristine chemistry offers researchers a rare, uncontaminated look at how space rocks may have delivered the essential building blocks of life to early Earth.
