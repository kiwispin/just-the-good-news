For the first time, researchers have documented the lifespan of a wild female Sunda clouded leopard, capturing camera trap footage of an 8.5-year-old huntress in Borneo. The remarkably healthy feline represents the oldest known individual of her species in the wild, offering conservationists invaluable new insights into these rare and beautiful big cats.
