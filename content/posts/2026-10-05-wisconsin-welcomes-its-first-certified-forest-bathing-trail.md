Wisconsin has officially opened its first certified Shinrin-yoku trail, offering visitors a dedicated space to practice the restorative art of forest bathing. Designed to be accessible to everyone, the trail encourages people to slow down, disconnect from daily distractions, and immerse their senses in the healing power of nature.
