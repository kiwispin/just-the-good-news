Scientists have successfully used probiotic and post-biotic treatments in the open ocean for the first time to revive heat-damaged coral in the Red Sea. Building on previous successes in aquariums and coastal Florida, this breakthrough offers a promising new tool for marine biologists working to protect wild reef ecosystems globally.
