Researchers at Johns Hopkins University have discovered that a vitamin A derivative collaborates with thyroid hormones to form the retina's center for sharp vision before birth. This breakthrough, made using lab-grown human retinas, overturns decades of scientific assumptions and offers new insights into human eye development. The discovery opens promising avenues for understanding and potentially treating vision disorders earlier in life.
