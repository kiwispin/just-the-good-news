New seismic data has revealed vast, ancient magmatic systems deep beneath the surface of Mars, showing that the Red Planet had a rich and active geological history similar to Earth's. This exciting discovery reshapes our understanding of planetary evolution and opens up inspiring new possibilities for how rocky planets form and develop over time.
