According to Brazil's official monitoring data, Amazon deforestation alerts fell by 36% over the past year, marking the lowest level recorded since 2013. The positive trend, driven by strengthened environmental enforcement, was also mirrored in other crucial Brazilian biomes like the Cerrado, which hit a five-year low.
