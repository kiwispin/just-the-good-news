A clinical trial involving nearly 1,000 patients across the UK revealed that a non-invasive, at-home urine test successfully identified 92 percent of bladder cancer cases. Additionally, a negative result offered over a 99 percent certainty that the patient was cancer-free, showcasing a major step forward in accessible healthcare.
