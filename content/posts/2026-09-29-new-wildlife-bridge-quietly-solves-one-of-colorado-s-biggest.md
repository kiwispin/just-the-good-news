Since its completion, North America’s largest wildlife overpass has reduced vehicle-animal collisions on a busy stretch of Interstate 25 by an impressive 91 percent. The structure has transformed a dangerous migration corridor, which previously averaged one deer collision per day, into a safe passage for both local wildlife and motorists.
