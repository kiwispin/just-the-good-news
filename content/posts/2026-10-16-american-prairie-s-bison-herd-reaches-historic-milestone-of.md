For the first time in over two decades of conservation efforts, the free-roaming bison herd at American Prairie has expanded to more than 1,000 animals. This achievement marks a significant step forward for the largest private land conservation initiative in the United States. The milestone highlights the ongoing ecological restoration of North America's Great Plains.
