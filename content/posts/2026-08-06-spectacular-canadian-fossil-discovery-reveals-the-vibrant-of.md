Researchers in Canada have uncovered a remarkably preserved 567-million-year-old fossil site, offering a fascinating window into our planet's earliest complex ecosystems. The discovery pushes back key evolutionary milestones by 10 million years, revealing that early animal life was already moving, reproducing, and thriving in the deep ocean much earlier than previously thought.
