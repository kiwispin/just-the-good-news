A woman in southern Italy who accidentally threw away a lottery ticket worth one million euros has been reunited with her winnings thanks to the dedication of local waste management workers. The crew from the SANB sanitation company spent hours searching through an entire truck of garbage to successfully locate the ticket fully intact.
