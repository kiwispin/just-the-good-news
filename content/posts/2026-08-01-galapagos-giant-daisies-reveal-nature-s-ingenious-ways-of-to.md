Scientists studying giant daisies in the Galápagos Islands have discovered that different populations are independently evolving similar heat-tolerant leaves using entirely different genetic pathways. This remarkable genetic diversity suggests that new plant species are actively forming right now, showcasing nature's resilient ability to adapt to changing environments.
