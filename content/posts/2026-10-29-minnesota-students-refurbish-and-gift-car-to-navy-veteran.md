Engineering students in Minnesota teamed up with the National Auto Body Council to restore a 2020 Hyundai Elantra for US Navy veteran Robert Hanson. After years without personal transportation, Hanson now has a reliable vehicle to help him navigate his daily life. The initiative highlights the power of hands-on student learning benefiting local community members in need.
