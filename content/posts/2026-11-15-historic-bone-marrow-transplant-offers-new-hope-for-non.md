Physicians at Franciscan Health have achieved a medical breakthrough by using bone marrow from a deceased donor to successfully treat a patient with aplastic anemia. This pioneering procedure marks the first time a non-malignant disease has been treated using this donor method, opening up vital new treatment options for patients with chronic blood conditions.
