Researchers have discovered experimental metal alloys that adapt their internal structures to absorb stress at temperatures as low as -424°F. Instead of becoming brittle and cracking like standard materials, these resilient metals reorganize to stay flexible in extreme environments. This breakthrough opens up safer possibilities for deep-space exploration and advanced industrial infrastructure.
