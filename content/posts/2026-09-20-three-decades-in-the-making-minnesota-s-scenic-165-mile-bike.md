After more than 30 years of dedicated planning, construction, and fundraising, the Mesabi Trail has officially been completed in northern Minnesota. This fully paved 165-mile pathway connects the Mississippi River to the edge of the Boundary Waters wilderness, offering cyclists and outdoor enthusiasts one of the longest continuous scenic trails in the United States.
