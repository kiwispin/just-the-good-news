A groundbreaking study published in The Lancet Public Health has revealed that reducing air pollution does more than just prevent harm—it actively helps children's lungs grow faster and healthier. Researchers tracking the impact of London's clean-air initiatives have provided the first concrete evidence that cleaning up urban air can successfully reverse the negative trajectory of childhood lung development.
