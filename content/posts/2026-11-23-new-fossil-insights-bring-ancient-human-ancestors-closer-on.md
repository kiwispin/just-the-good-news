Researchers at Monash University are proposing a unified view of early human evolution, suggesting ancient ancestors long thought to be separate species actually belong in our shared genus, Homo. This reclassification highlights a deeper, more interconnected heritage between modern humans and our earliest relatives. As more discoveries come to light, scientists are uncovering a richer and more inclusive story of human origins.
