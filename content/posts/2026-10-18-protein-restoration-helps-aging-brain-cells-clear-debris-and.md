Researchers have discovered that restoring a youth-associated protein in aging brain cells helps restore their natural protective abilities. In mouse studies, boosting levels of this protein enabled immune cells in the brain to clear cellular waste more effectively while reducing markers of age-related inflammation. The findings offer a promising foundation for developing future therapies to support healthy cognitive aging and manage neurodegenerative conditions.
