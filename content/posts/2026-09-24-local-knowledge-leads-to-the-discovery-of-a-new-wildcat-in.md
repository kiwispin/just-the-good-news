Biologists studying Bolivia's Yungas cloud forest have officially identified the tilcayo, a distinct new species of wildcat. Long categorized broadly as a tiger cat by researchers, the feline was recognized as unique thanks to the traditional knowledge of local communities who have always known it by its distinct name.
