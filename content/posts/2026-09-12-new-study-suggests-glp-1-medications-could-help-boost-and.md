Researchers have discovered that semaglutide, the active ingredient in drugs like Ozempic, extended the lifespan of healthy mice while enhancing their memory, muscle function, and blood sugar regulation. The findings suggest these treatments may tap into unique biological pathways that actively slow the aging process, offering promising new avenues for healthy aging in humans.
