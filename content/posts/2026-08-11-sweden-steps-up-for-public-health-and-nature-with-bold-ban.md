Starting January 1, 2028, Sweden will officially ban harmful per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances (PFAS) from a wide range of everyday consumer products, including clothing, cosmetics, and kitchenware. This decisive legislative move represents a major milestone in safeguarding both human health and the Swedish environment from persistent chemical pollutants.
