Scientists exploring the flooded forests of the western Amazon have successfully identified a brand-new frog species, Adenomera varcena, which had previously gone unnoticed due to its resemblance to near-identical relatives. This exciting discovery, made possible through advanced genetic analysis and acoustic tracking, showcases the incredible, resilient biodiversity still waiting to be found in our planet's rainforests.
