Scientists have developed a sustainable new material that achieves intense whiteness and water resistance by mimicking the natural structures of snow and lotus leaves. By using tiny pores to scatter light and repel water, this innovation eliminates the need for both titanium dioxide pigments and harmful PFAS 'forever chemicals.' This breakthrough paves the way for eco-friendly everyday products, from paints to packaging, that are safer for both people and the planet.
