A newly launched biomethane plant in Grottole, Italy, is transforming local agricultural waste into renewable gas for more than 3,000 homes. Generating 45 gigawatt-hours of energy annually, the facility is projected to prevent roughly 9,000 tons of carbon dioxide emissions each year.
