While thawing Arctic permafrost is releasing ancient carbon into the ocean, researchers have discovered a reassuring natural safeguard. Study of sediment cores near Canada's Herschel Island reveals that the seafloor safely buries about 90 percent of this land-based carbon, preventing it from turning into greenhouse gases.
