Concentrations of the harmful forever chemicals PFOS and PFOA have dropped by nearly 30 percent in England's rivers over the past decade, according to new data from the Environment Agency. The positive trend extends to wildlife, with chemical levels in freshwater fish falling by 49 percent following regulatory bans.
