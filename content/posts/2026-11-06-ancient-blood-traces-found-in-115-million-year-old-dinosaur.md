Researchers have discovered chemically confirmed traces of ancient blood within 115-million-year-old dinosaur bones, reshaping our understanding of how long biological molecules can endure. This remarkable preservation offers scientists an unprecedented look into dinosaur physiology and provides valuable new techniques for detecting signs of ancient life on other planets.
