After years of dedicated service to the Aubrey Independent School District, career custodian Marilyn Hampton received the ultimate tribute from her community. A brand-new school in the district now bears her name, honoring her decades of devotion and hard work. Hampton expressed overwhelming gratitude for the rare recognition, which she described as greater than anything she had ever imagined.
