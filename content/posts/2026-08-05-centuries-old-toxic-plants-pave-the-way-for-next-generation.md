Researchers have successfully synthesized the therapeutic properties of wolfsbane and larkspur, two famously toxic plants historically used in traditional medicine. This breakthrough allows scientists to harness the plants' natural pain-fighting and cancer-treating compounds without the associated toxins, opening the door for powerful new drug therapies.
