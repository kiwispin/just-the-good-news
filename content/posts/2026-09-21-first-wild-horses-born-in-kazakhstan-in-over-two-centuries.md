The birth of two Przewalski’s horse foals in central Kazakhstan marks a monumental milestone for wildlife restoration, as they are the first of their species born on Kazakh soil in more than 200 years. This hopeful development follows a dedicated reintroduction program aimed at returning these endangered wild horses to their ancestral grassland habitats.
