Kansas Teacher's Daily Affirmation Ritual Transforms Elementary School Culture

Good News Network 23 October 2026

Photo: Far Chinberdiev / Unsplash

First-grade teacher Tyler Richards started a daily routine where students chant uplifting affirmations like 'I am kind' and 'I can do hard things' before heading home. The simple practice has proved so impactful that the positive mindset ritual is now spreading across the entire school.
