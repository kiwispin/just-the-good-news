Over 1,000 Swiss farmers and gardeners participated in a national study to measure soil fertility using a simple, eco-friendly method: burying pure cotton underwear. By observing how quickly the natural fabric decomposed, researchers gathered valuable, crowd-sourced data to help improve soil health and crop sustainability.
