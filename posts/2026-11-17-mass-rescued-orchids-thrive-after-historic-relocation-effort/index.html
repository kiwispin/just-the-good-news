Mass Rescued Orchids Thrive After Historic Relocation Effort in Southern India

Mongabay 17 November 2026

Photo: Tuhin Santra / Unsplash

A large-scale conservation effort successfully saved tens of thousands of epiphytic orchids from a road construction corridor in southern India. A recent study confirms the success of the operation, which relocated the delicate plants onto safe host trees to preserve the region's rich botanical diversity.
