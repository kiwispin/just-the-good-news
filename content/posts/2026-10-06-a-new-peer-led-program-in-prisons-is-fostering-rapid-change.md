Founded by formerly incarcerated advocates Frank Davis and Aaron Hicks, the ECHO program—Every Choice Holds an Opportunity—is helping incarcerated men transform their mindsets and behaviors in just nine weeks. By leveraging lived experience and peer mentorship, the initiative is successfully fostering personal accountability and supportive communities within the correctional system.
