New Pet Weather App Measures Ground Temps to Keep Dogs Safe on Walks

Good News Network 2 November 2026

Photo: Abid Shah / Unsplash

A dog lover has launched Weather.pet, an app designed to protect pets by providing ground-level temperature forecasts for asphalt, grass, and concrete. The service delivers tailored reports based on a dog's breed and size, helping owners plan safe daily walks away from dangerously hot pavement.
