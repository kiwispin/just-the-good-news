Fossil Discovery in Colombia Reveals Life Can Flourish and Evolve Without Mass Extinction

ScienceDaily 10 November 2026

Photo: Eduardo Aguilera Ruiz / Unsplash

Researchers in Colombia have uncovered a vibrant ancient marine ecosystem that demonstrates how new species can thrive alongside older ones during major evolutionary shifts. The discovery introduces the concept of a 'Biotic Inflection,' offering inspiring evidence that Earth's biodiversity can transform through gradual, harmonious transitions rather than catastrophic events.
