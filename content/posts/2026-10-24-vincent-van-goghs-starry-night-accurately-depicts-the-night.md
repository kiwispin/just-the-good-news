A new astronomical analysis reveals that the celestial patterns in Vincent van Gogh's masterwork 'Starry Night' precisely match the actual night sky above France in July 1889. The discovery offers a fascinating link between science and art, showing that the famous canvas reflects a genuine historical moment in the night sky.
