Researchers have discovered that honeybees can actively detect nutrient imbalances in pollen and adjust their consumption to avoid harmful excesses. In a remarkable display of natural wisdom, they also process this pollen into a perfectly balanced 'baby food' to ensure their larvae get the exact nutrition they need to thrive.
