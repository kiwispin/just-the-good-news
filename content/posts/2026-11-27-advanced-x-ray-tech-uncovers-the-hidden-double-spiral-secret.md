An international team of scientists has used state-of-the-art X-ray imaging to unlock a long-held secret of the narwhal's iconic tusk. They discovered that the structure contains two microscopic, counter-twisting spirals, revealing a masterpiece of natural engineering that gives the tusk its unique strength and flexibility. This breakthrough deepens our appreciation for ocean wildlife and the incredible wonders of natural evolution.
