Researchers have repurposed the lightweight material found in emergency blankets to create an exceptionally strong thermal insulator for space exploration. By layering the thin plastic, scientists developed a durable barrier capable of withstanding intense pressure and extreme temperatures, offering a promising step forward for long-term Moon habitats.
