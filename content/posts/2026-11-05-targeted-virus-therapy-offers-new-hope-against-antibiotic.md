An international team of scientists has published the first standardized guidelines for personalized phage therapy, a promising treatment that uses natural viruses to destroy harmful bacteria. Recent clinical trials demonstrate encouraging progress, with 77% of treated patients showing health improvements and targeted bacteria eliminated in over six out of ten cases.
