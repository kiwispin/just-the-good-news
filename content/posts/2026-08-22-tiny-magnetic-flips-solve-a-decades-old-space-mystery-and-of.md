By discovering that protons and neutrons flip their internal magnets inside atomic nuclei, scientists have finally explained a long-standing mystery involving low-energy gamma rays. This breakthrough will significantly improve our understanding of nuclear reactions on Earth and how heavy elements are forged during spectacular neutron star mergers.
