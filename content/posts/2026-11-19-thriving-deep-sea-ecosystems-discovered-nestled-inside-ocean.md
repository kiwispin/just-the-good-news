Scientists exploring the deep Pacific Ocean have revealed that slow-forming, metal-rich rocks serve as vibrant habitats for thousands of small marine creatures. The discovery highlights the surprising richness of deep-sea life, with over 4,100 animals found coexisting in unique micro-communities within these natural shelters.
