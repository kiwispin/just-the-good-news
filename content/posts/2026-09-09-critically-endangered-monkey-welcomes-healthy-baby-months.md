An extraordinary veterinary procedure has saved a roloway monkey's foot from amputation, paving the way for her to give birth to a healthy daughter named Lagertha. This successful birth represents a vital boost for the critically endangered species, which is estimated to have fewer than 2,000 individuals remaining in the wild.
