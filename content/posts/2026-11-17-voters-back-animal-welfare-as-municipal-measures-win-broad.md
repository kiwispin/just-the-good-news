In recent midterm elections, voters in major US cities overwhelmingly approved new protections for animal welfare, including a ban on foie gras sales in Washington, DC, that passed with 73 percent of the vote. The strong turnout reflects a growing nationwide commitment to humane food sourcing and animal care standards.
