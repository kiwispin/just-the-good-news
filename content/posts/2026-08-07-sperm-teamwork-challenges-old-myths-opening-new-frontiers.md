A sweeping evolutionary study has revealed that sperm often cooperate in coordinated teams rather than competing individually. This discovery of widespread collaboration among arthropods challenges the traditional 'survival of the fittest' narrative and could pave the way for breakthrough developments in both fertility research and eco-friendly pest control.
