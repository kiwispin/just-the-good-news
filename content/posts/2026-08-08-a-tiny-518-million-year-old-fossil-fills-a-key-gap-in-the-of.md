The discovery of beautifully preserved soft tissue and pincers in a half-billion-year-old marine fossil has finally revealed how ancient creatures evolved the specialized fangs used by modern arachnids. This remarkable find provides scientists with a long-sought evolutionary link, shedding new light on the history of life on Earth.
