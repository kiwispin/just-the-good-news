Three years after an avian flu outbreak wiped out the local population, dedicated conservation efforts on England's south coast have helped sandwich terns rebound from zero to 756 birds. The thriving sanctuary has also welcomed back several other seabird species, including little terns, ringed plovers, and oystercatchers.
