A collaborative study by Michigan State University and the University of Kentucky reveals that strong friendships within the LGBTQ+ community significantly boost mental well-being. Researchers observed lower rates of anxiety and depression among individuals with robust peer support networks. The findings underscore the vital role that inclusive, affirming social connections play in fostering long-term emotional health.
