Researchers have discovered that the unique iron-rich soil of Western Australia's Pilbara region naturally generates hydrogen, with the potential to significantly boost production. If scaled up successfully, these vast subterranean formations could provide a massive, low-emission energy source for both Australia and the global shift toward green power.
