Researchers have discovered that tiny dwarf reed snakes can roll across the ground at speeds up to four times faster than slithering. By coordinating precise body rotations while keeping their heads steady, these remarkably agile reptiles have developed a unique and efficient escape mechanism. The finding highlights the incredible adaptability and diverse locomotion strategies found in nature.
