In a major scientific breakthrough, the James Webb Space Telescope has detected strong evidence of an atmosphere on the lava-covered planet HD 3167 b, despite its extreme heat and close orbit to its star. This surprising discovery offers researchers a rare window into how rocky planets retain their atmospheres, providing vital clues about the molten conditions of early Earth.
