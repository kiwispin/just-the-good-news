Using innovative protein analysis on ancient teeth, scientists have uncovered new insights into Homo naledi, identifying an all-female fossil site dating back over 240,000 years. The discovery resolves a long-standing archaeological puzzle and offers a fascinating new window into the social structures of early human relatives.
