By introducing a feast-and-fast feeding schedule with whole carcasses, researchers successfully encouraged zoo lions to exhibit more natural, wild behaviors. The change resulted in longer, more active feeding sessions followed by deep rest, closely mimicking the healthy activity patterns seen in the wild.
