State wildlife officials are scaling up Maine's successful network of underground tunnels, allowing larger mammals like bears and moose to bypass traffic safely. The expansion builds on the proven success of smaller crossings, which have already helped local turtles, foxes, and other small animals navigate fragmented habitats. This proactive infrastructure boost protects migrating animals while significantly reducing the risk of collisions for drivers.
