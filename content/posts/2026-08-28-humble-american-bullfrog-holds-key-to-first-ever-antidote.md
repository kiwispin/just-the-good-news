Scientists have discovered that the American bullfrog produces a unique protein capable of neutralizing saxitoxin, a lethal neurotoxin found in contaminated shellfish. This natural defense mechanism could lead to the first effective treatment for thousands of people affected by paralytic shellfish poisoning each year.
