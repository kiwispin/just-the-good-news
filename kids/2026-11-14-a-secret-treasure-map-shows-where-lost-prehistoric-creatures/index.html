A Secret Treasure Map Shows Where Lost Prehistoric Creatures Are Hiding!

Saturday 14 November 2026 •ScienceDaily

Photo: Narciso Arellano / Unsplash

Scientists have created an incredible new map that points to hidden places all around the world where ancient sea creatures lay buried. You might think the oldest rocks hide the best secrets, but this map shows that younger rocks from more recent times are actually packed with undiscovered fossils [the hardened remains of ancient plants and animals]. Who knows what kind of bizarre, real-life sea monsters are waiting for us to dig them up?
