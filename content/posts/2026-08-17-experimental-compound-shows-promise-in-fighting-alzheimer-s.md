Scientists have successfully tested a new compound in mice that prevents harmful protein clumps from forming in the brain, helping nerve cells survive longer and reducing amyloid buildup. Beyond protecting brain health, the treatment also showed encouraging side benefits, including improved heart health and slowed signs of aging.
