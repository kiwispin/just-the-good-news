Scientists at the Mayo Clinic have published a series of studies showing how targeting lingering, damaged cells can significantly reduce inflammation and support overall health. Experimental treatments in animal models—including the use of a natural compound found in tomatoes—helped improve both physical and brain function, paving the way for therapies that promote healthier aging.
