Dutch startup Respyre has developed 'Mosscrete,' a unique bioreceptive concrete coating that encourages moss to grow naturally on vertical building surfaces. This innovative material turns ordinary urban structures into self-sustaining green facades, helping to cool cities and enhance urban biodiversity.
