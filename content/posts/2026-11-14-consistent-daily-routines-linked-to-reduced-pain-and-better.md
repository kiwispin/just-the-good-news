A study from the University of Missouri reveals that maintaining regular times for meals, sleep, and daily activities can significantly reduce physical pain and depression in older adults. Researchers found that these consistent habits help stabilize the body's internal clock, offering meaningful health benefits even for those experiencing insomnia.
