Researchers have resolved a 2,000-year-old mystery by confirming that the Qumran sect successfully used a unique, perfectly ordered 364-day calendar to keep holidays on the exact same weekdays. This breakthrough provides historians with a clearer, more harmonious understanding of ancient timekeeping and daily life during the era the scrolls were written.
