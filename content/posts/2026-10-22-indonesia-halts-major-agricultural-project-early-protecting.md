Indonesian authorities have paused a massive land conversion initiative after achieving national rice self-sufficiency three years ahead of schedule. The decision leaves roughly four million acres of critical rainforests, peatlands, and swamps undisturbed across several islands. While long-term plans for the land are still being finalized, the unexpected milestone provides vital relief for local ecosystems.
