Japanese researchers have found that a natural compound derived from soy, known as LPC70, helped shield mice from the negative cognitive and cardiovascular effects of a high-salt diet. Animals given the supplement demonstrated improved memory, better social interactions, and reduced blood pressure. The findings offer a promising potential avenue for mitigating the health risks associated with excess dietary sodium.
