South African Vineyard Welcomes Flock of 1,000 Ducks as Natural Pest Control

Good Good Good 3 September 2026

Photo: Werner Du plessis / Unsplash

By adopting a centuries-old Indonesian farming tradition, a South African winery has replaced chemical pesticides with a highly effective flock of runner ducks. These feathered workers naturally protect the grapevines from pests while providing organic fertilizer, creating a sustainable and chemical-free ecosystem for wine production.
