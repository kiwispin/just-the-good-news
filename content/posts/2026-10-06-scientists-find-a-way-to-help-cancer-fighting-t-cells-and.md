Researchers at Memorial Sloan Kettering Cancer Center have discovered that blocking a specific signaling molecule prevents crucial immune cells from burning out. By conserving their energy, these modified T cells can survive longer and remain highly active in targeting tumors, offering a promising boost to immunotherapy treatments.
