The Empire State Building was illuminated in gold to honor NASA's Voyager 1 reaching a distance of one light-day from Earth. The golden glow pays tribute to the spacecraft's iconic Golden Record as it continues its historic journey into interstellar space, now requiring a full 24 hours to transmit signals back home.
