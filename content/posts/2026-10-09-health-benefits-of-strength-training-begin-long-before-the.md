A new study reveals that just seven resistance training sessions prompted obese mice to shrink deep abdominal fat cells and reactivate fat-burning pathways. These findings suggest that the positive cellular impacts of exercise begin almost immediately, offering encouragement that workouts are boosting health even before visible weight loss occurs.
