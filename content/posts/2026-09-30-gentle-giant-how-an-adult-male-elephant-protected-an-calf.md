An orphaned seven-month-old elephant calf was successfully rescued by wildlife rangers after a protective bull elephant 'adopted' her and kept her safe from predators. While the male elephant could not provide the milk she needed to survive, his temporary guardianship kept her alive long enough for conservationists to intervene and transport her to a specialized rehabilitation center.
