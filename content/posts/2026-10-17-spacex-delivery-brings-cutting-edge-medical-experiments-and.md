A recent supply mission to the International Space Station has delivered advanced scientific experiments, including 3D heart tissue, miniature brain models, and tech for producing artificial retinas. Researchers hope conducting these tests in microgravity will accelerate new treatments for brain diseases and vision loss on Earth.
