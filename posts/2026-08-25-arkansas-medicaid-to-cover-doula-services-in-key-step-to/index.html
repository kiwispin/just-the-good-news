Arkansas Medicaid to Cover Doula Services in Key Step to Improve Maternal Health

Good Good Good 25 August 2026

Photo: Jametlene Reskp / Unsplash

Starting in September 2026, Arkansas Medicaid will officially cover doula care, complying with a state law designed to support new and expecting mothers. This policy expansion aims to improve maternal health outcomes by making continuous, personalized birth support accessible to more families.
