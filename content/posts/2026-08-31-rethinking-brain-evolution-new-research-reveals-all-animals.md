Scientists have debunked the long-standing myth of a primitive 'lizard brain,' discovering that brain evolution is actually a highly adaptable balancing act of two sophisticated wiring styles. This breakthrough reshapes our understanding of the animal kingdom, showing that even the simplest creatures possess beautifully complex and customized brains designed for survival.
