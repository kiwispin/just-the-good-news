For the first time, researchers have demonstrated that human heart muscle cells can actually regenerate after a heart attack, challenging a long-held belief that such damage was permanent. This groundbreaking discovery could pave the way for innovative new treatments that enhance the heart's natural repair process and help prevent heart failure.
