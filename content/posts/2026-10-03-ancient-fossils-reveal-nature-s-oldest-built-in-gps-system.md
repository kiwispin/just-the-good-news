Scientists have discovered 97-million-year-old magnetic fossils that represent the earliest known evidence of animals using Earth's magnetic field for navigation. This remarkable find reveals that prehistoric ocean creatures possessed a highly sophisticated, natural GPS system to travel thousands of kilometers with incredible precision.
