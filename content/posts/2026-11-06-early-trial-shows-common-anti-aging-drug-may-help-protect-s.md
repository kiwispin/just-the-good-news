A new study reveals that a short course of low-dose rapamycin significantly improved brain blood flow in healthy adults carrying a high-risk gene for Alzheimer's disease. Because reduced circulation often precedes memory loss by years, researchers hope this early intervention could help prevent or delay the onset of the condition.
