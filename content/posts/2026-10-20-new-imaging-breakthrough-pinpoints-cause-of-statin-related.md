Researchers have discovered the specific cellular mechanism that causes muscle discomfort in some statin users, using advanced imaging to see how the drugs interact with calcium channels in muscle cells. This clearer understanding offers a promising path toward developing side-effect-free cholesterol treatments and improving quality of life for millions of heart health patients.
