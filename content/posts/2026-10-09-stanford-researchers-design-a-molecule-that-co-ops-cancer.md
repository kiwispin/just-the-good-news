Scientists have successfully developed a novel molecule that transforms a cancer-promoting protein into a pathway for self-destruction. In laboratory tests, this innovative approach completely eliminated aggressive human lymphoma tumors in mice within just 11 days, paving the way for a promising new class of targeted cancer therapies.
