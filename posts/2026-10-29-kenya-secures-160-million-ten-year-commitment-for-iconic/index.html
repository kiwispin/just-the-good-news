Kenya Secures $160 Million Ten-Year Commitment for Iconic Biodiversity Landscapes

The Optimist Daily 29 October 2026

Photo: Thomas Bennie / Unsplash

Kenya has launched the decade-long Thamani Asili program, dedicating $160 million to safeguard 173,000 square kilometers of vital African ecosystems. The initiative focuses on protecting critical biodiversity hotspots, including the Great Rift Valley and the Maasai Mara, ensuring the preservation of some of the continent's most pristine natural habitats.
