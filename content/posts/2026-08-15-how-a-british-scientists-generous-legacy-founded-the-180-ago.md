On this day in 1840, the Smithsonian Institution was officially chartered thanks to a $500,000 bequest from British scientist James Smithson, who had never even visited the United States. His visionary gift established a world-class network of museums and research complexes dedicated to the advancement and diffusion of human knowledge.
