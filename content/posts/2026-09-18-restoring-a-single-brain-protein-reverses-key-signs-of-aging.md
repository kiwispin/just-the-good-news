Researchers have discovered that boosting levels of the brain protein Menin can reverse age-related cognitive decline, bone loss, and skin thinning in mice. Additionally, supplementing with the amino acid D-serine was shown to significantly improve cognitive function, offering a promising new pathway for healthy aging therapies.
