In a fascinating discovery off the Florida coast, scientists found that wild blacktip sharks can detect and precisely locate low-frequency sounds from nearly 250 feet away. This research sheds new light on shark sensory capabilities, helping conservationists better understand how these marine predators navigate and interact with their underwater environment.
