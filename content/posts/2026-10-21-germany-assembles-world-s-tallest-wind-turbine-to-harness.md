Engineering firm GICON has completed construction on a record-breaking 365-meter wind turbine in Schipkau, Germany. Reaching heights nearly equal to Berlin's iconic TV tower, the pilot project is designed to tap into stronger, more consistent upper-atmosphere winds to boost clean energy generation when it goes live this November.
