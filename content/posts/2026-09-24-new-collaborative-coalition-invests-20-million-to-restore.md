A new corporate coalition led by Amazon has launched a $20 million initiative to fund critical water conservation projects, including irrigation upgrades and leak repairs, along the drought-stressed Colorado River. The effort aims to improve water security and climate resilience for the millions of people and ecosystems that rely on the basin.
