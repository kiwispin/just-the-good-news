Recent scientific research shows that consistent physical activity does more than build muscle—it actively rewires the neural network controlling the heart. Researchers discovered that these positive structural changes occur distinctly on the left and right sides of the organ, offering a deeper understanding of how staying active optimizes cardiovascular health.
