Researchers at the University of Manchester have developed a highly accurate, non-invasive test that uses urine and vaginal fluid samples to screen for womb cancer. This inexpensive breakthrough correctly identified 93 percent of cancer-free patients, potentially sparing millions of women from painful and invasive diagnostic procedures.
