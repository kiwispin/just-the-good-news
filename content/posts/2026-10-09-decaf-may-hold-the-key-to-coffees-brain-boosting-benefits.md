A new study has revealed that participants who drank decaffeinated coffee showed significant improvements on memory tests, while those drinking caffeinated coffee did not. These findings suggest that the cognitive benefits associated with your morning brew may come from natural compounds in the coffee bean itself rather than the caffeine.
