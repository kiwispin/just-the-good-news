In a Stanford University laboratory, scientists observed a healthy coral fragment transferring beneficial algae to a neighboring bleached fragment after the two accidentally touched. This natural transfer allowed the stressed coral to begin recovering its color and vital nutrients within days, offering a hopeful new insight into marine ecosystem resilience.
