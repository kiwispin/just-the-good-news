Scientists at McGill University have revealed that T cells can sense the stiffness of surrounding tissues to help them transform into long-lasting memory cells. This landmark discovery offers a new way of understanding how the body builds lasting protection against future infections. The insights could pave the way for more effective vaccines and targeted immune therapies.
