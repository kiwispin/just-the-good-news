Geologists have uncovered evidence of a colossal ancient cliff system that formed during the breakup of the supercontinent Rodinia, offering a groundbreaking explanation for the Grand Canyon's famously missing geological layers. This remarkable discovery provides scientists with a long-sought key to understanding Earth's deep history and the origins of one of the world's most iconic landscapes.
