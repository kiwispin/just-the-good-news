Archaeologists studying 60,000-year-old engraved ostrich eggshells from southern Africa have discovered highly organized geometric patterns, including grids and right angles. These findings suggest that our early ancestors possessed a sophisticated ability for mental planning and artistic rules much earlier than previously thought.
