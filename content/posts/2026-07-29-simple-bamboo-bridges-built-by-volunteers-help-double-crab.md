Local volunteers in Taiwan have successfully doubled the population of migrating crabs by constructing simple, effective bamboo bridges to help them bypass dangerous roadways. Active during the July-to-September migration season, this community-led conservation effort showcases how basic materials and local care can dramatically protect coastal ecosystems.
