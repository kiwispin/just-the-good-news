Researchers studying the Australian jumping spider have uncovered evidence that these remarkable arachnids experience regulated sleep patterns similar to humans. By observing distinct sleep stages, including a REM-like state with eye movements and twitches, scientists have opened a delightful new window into how animals process their daily lives.
