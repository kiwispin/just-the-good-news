Documentarian Reunites 103-Year-Old D-Day Veteran With Lost WWII Medals

Good News Network 12 November 2026

Photo: David Clode / Unsplash

An amateur WWII historian helped a 103-year-old D-Day survivor recover his missing military honours after discovering the man's records during a research project. The long-lost medals were safely returned to the veteran and his family just in time for Veterans Day.
