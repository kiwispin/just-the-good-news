Six Newborn Calves Bring Hope to Cambodia's Sacred Mekong River Dolphins

Good News Network 26 October 2026

Photo: Andy Kennedy / Unsplash

The critically endangered Irrawaddy dolphin population along Cambodia's Mekong River has welcomed six new calves following a successful breeding season. Revered by local communities, these rare freshwater dolphins are seeing a hopeful boost in numbers thanks to ongoing conservation efforts in the region.
