Clever Meerkats Have a Secret Built-In Compass!

Thursday 26 November 2026 •Newsround (BBC)

Photo: Alan J. Hendry / Unsplash

Even if a little meerkat gets lost all on its own, it can easily find its way back to its family burrow without any help at all! These amazing desert animals use the sun and landmarks (like familiar rocks and trees) to navigate their way home like tiny furry explorers. Isn't it incredible to think that such a small creature has such a big sense of direction?
