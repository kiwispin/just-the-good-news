A new study reveals that staying physically active and maintaining healthy blood sugar levels during middle age can significantly slow cognitive decline decades later. Researchers also found that physical activity offered particularly strong brain-protective benefits for Mexican American adults, highlighting the importance of tailored wellness approaches.
