Scientists have discovered that superheating magma can delay crystal formation for hours, providing key insights into why certain volcanoes erupt into spectacular fountains rather than quiet flows. This new understanding helps researchers better model volcanic behavior, ultimately improving safety and prediction efforts around active sites worldwide.
