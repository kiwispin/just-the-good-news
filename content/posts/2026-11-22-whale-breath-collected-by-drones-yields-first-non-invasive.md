An international research team has successfully sequenced the complete genome of a wild whale using genetic material gathered entirely from its exhaled breath. Captured by overhead drones, this groundbreaking technique allows marine scientists to gather crucial biological data without disturbing the ocean giants. The non-invasive method opens up promising new avenues for studying and protecting marine wildlife worldwide.
