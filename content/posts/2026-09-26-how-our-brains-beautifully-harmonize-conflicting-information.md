Scientists have discovered a built-in 'consensus building' mechanism in the brain that quickly resolves disagreements between different visual areas. By keeping matching signals active while letting conflicting ones quickly fade, the brain naturally simplifies complex inputs to give us a unified, harmonious view of the world.
