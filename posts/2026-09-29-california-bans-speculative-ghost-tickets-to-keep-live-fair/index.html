California Bans Speculative 'Ghost Tickets' to Keep Live Events Fair and Affordable

Good Good Good 29 September 2026

Photo: Leo_Visions / Unsplash

A newly enacted California law protects event-goers by banning the sale of speculative 'ghost tickets' that sellers do not actually own. This consumer-first legislation aims to curb artificial price inflation, ensuring fans have more transparent and affordable access to their favorite concerts and sporting events.
