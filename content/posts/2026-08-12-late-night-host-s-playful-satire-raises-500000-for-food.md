Comedian John Oliver raised half a million dollars to fight food insecurity by opening a cheeky pop-up shop called 'Buc Off' in Texas. The proceeds from the satirical store, which poked fun at a major convenience store chain's legal disputes with small businesses, are being donated entirely to local food banks.
