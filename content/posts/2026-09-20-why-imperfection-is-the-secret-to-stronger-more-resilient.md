Physicists have discovered that complex systems, from power grids to natural ecosystems, actually become more stable when their components have slight, diverse differences rather than being perfectly uniform. This breakthrough offers a blueprint for designing more resilient infrastructure and helps explain the strength found in nature's inherent diversity.
