A new study reveals that people carrying a growth hormone receptor gene inherited from Neanderthals have, on average, hundreds of additional grams of muscle mass. Those who inherited the variant from both parents see even greater muscle development, shedding light on how ancient genetics continue to shape human physical strength today.
