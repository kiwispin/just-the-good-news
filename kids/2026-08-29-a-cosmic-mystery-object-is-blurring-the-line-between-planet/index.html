A Cosmic Mystery Object Is Blurring the Line Between Planet and Moon!

Saturday 29 August 2026 •Science News for Students

Photo: Javier Miranda / Unsplash

Astronomers have spotted a bizarre space world orbiting a brown dwarf (a giant object that is bigger than a planet but smaller than a star). This strange world is so unusual that scientists aren't sure if they should call it a giant moon, a toddler planet, or something completely new. What would you name a world that behaves like both a planet and a moon?
