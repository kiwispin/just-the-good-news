Researchers have identified that deactivating a protein known as Mitch encourages human cells to burn existing fat while preventing new fat cells from forming. Building on successful preclinical trials that also demonstrated enhanced muscular endurance, this discovery offers a promising new avenue for weight-loss therapies.
