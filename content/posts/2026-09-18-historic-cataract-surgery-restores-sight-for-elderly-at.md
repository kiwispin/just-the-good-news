Pierre, a 54-year-old chimpanzee living at Chimp Haven, has successfully regained his vision after undergoing the sanctuary's first-ever cataract surgery. Following a period of complete blindness, the landmark procedure has allowed Pierre to quickly rejoin his community and reconnect with his fellow chimpanzees and caregivers.
