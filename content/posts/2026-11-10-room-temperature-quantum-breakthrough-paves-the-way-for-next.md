Scientists have successfully observed a rare state of matter that flows like a frictionless liquid while maintaining a crystal structure, achieving this feat at room temperature for the first time. By overcoming previous extreme-cooling limitations, this discovery brings ultra-efficient light-based computing and advanced sensing technologies much closer to practical everyday use.
