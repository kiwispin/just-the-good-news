Scientists have discovered that specialized brain cells called astrocytes can actively rebuild damaged neural networks by creating and sending new nuclei to repopulate injured areas. This remarkable self-repair mechanism, observed in study models, reveals that the adult brain possesses a much stronger capacity for healing than previously understood.
