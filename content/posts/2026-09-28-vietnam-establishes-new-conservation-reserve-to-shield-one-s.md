Vietnam is establishing the Kim Bang Delacour’s Langur Habitat Conservation Area, dedicating approximately 7,500 acres of pristine forest and limestone karst mountains to protect the critically endangered monkey. This swift conservation action secures a vital sanctuary for the species, ensuring their natural habitat remains safeguarded for the future.
