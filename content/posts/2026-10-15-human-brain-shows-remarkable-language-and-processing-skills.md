Researchers at Baylor University have discovered that the human brain remains surprisingly active during general anesthesia, continuing to process language, recognize word types, and anticipate storylines. This revelation expands our understanding of cognitive resilience and opens new avenues for exploring human consciousness. The findings suggest the unconscious mind is significantly more capable than previously thought.
