Researchers have identified a biological link between obesity-related fat molecules and Alzheimer's progression, marking a major step forward in understanding the disease. Crucially, scientists successfully restored memory and brain function in laboratory models by simply correcting this fat imbalance, opening the door to potential new targeted treatments.
