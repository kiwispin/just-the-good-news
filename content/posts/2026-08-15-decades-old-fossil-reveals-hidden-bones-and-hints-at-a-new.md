Paleontologists analyzing a 30-year-old fossil from Osaka, Japan, have discovered four previously unrecognized bones belonging to a giant Cretaceous mosasaur. Among the finds is a rare jawbone never before seen in Japanese specimens, alongside unique anatomical features that suggest the discovery of an entirely new ancient species.
