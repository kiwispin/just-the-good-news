A Phase 1 trial conducted by the Cleveland Clinic demonstrates that a one-time CRISPR gene-editing therapy successfully lowered dangerous LDL cholesterol levels by 52 percent. Patients experienced no side effects and maintained the significant reduction 12 months after the initial treatment, offering a promising new approach to long-term cardiovascular health.
