Researchers at the Princeton Plasma Physics Laboratory have discovered a new method to streamline fusion energy production by adjusting how plasma is heated and compressed. By reversing the traditional approach, future reactors could achieve self-sustaining power using significantly less energy. This mathematical breakthrough brings the prospect of virtually unlimited, zero-carbon energy one step closer to reality.
