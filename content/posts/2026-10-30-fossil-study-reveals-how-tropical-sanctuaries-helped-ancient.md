Scientists have uncovered new evidence explaining how ancient crocodiles survived the mass extinction event that wiped out the dinosaurs 66 million years ago. A comprehensive analysis of fossils and historical climate data shows that their affinity for warm, tropical environments provided natural havens that remained stable through global climate upheaval.
