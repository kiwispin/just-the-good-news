Researchers at the University of Nottingham have developed a wristband that uses mild electrical pulses to significantly reduce tics for individuals with Tourette's syndrome. The wearable device delivers near-instant stimulation to nerves in the wrist, providing a promising and non-invasive new tool to help manage symptoms.
