Researchers have discovered that chimpanzees repeatedly throw stones at specific trees to create enduring rock piles, a unique behavior that could represent a sophisticated form of symbolic communication. By using non-intrusive camera traps and audio recorders, scientists are now gaining a deeper, more respectful understanding of these extraordinary social traditions.
