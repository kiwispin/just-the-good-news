A dedicated team of volunteers and scientists at the Royal Botanic Gardens Victoria has successfully cultivated a thriving nursery of the endangered rosella spider orchid. The nursery now houses twice as many of these rare plants as are estimated to exist in the wild, establishing a vital safeguard against extinction.
