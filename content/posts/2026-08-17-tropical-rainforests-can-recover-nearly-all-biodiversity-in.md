A groundbreaking study from Ecuador’s highly biodiverse Chocó region reveals that cleared tropical rainforests can reclaim 90 percent of their original species diversity within a single human generation. By pooling data from over 30 research projects, scientists have demonstrated that these vital ecosystems possess a remarkable capacity for rapid natural recovery.
