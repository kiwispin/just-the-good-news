Residents in the Warwickshire village of Fillongley have combined crafting and community safety by knitting life-sized traffic officers and speed cameras. Members of the local Knit and Natter group dressed the figures in high-visibility vests, successfully encouraging passing motorists to slow down and protect pedestrians.
