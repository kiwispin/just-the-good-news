Researchers have discovered that a fascinating, ancient reshuffling of genome architecture helped soft-bodied cephalopods develop their famously complex brains. By mapping this 3D genetic structure, scientists are gaining a clearer picture of how advanced intelligence evolved in nature. This insight offers a valuable new lens for understanding the marvels of marine life and evolutionary biology.
