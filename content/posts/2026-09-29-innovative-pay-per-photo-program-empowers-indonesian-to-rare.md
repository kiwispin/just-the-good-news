In West Kalimantan, Borneo, a creative conservation initiative is turning local residents into wildlife protectors by offering supplemental income for capturing photos and audio of endangered species. Spanning nine different villages, this community-led approach provides a sustainable financial alternative to hunting while actively safeguarding local birds and orangutans.
