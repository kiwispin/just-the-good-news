In a major scientific milestone, researchers have expanded the Sloan Digital Sky Survey to the Southern Hemisphere, mapping over three million new cosmic objects. This rich new dataset is freely available to global researchers, unlocking discoveries of rare stars, glowing nebulae, and black holes to help us better understand our universe.
