Modern CT scans of rare dodo skulls are dispelling the myth of the clumsy, unintelligent bird by revealing they possessed a sophisticated sense of smell and highly sensitive beaks. This scientific breakthrough restores the reputation of the extinct species, showing they were actually complex, active creatures well-adapted to their environment.
