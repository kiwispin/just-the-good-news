A clinical trial led by University of Nottingham researchers has shown that a novel wrist device called Neupulse successfully reduced tic frequency by an average of 25 percent. Developed to deliver mild electrical stimulation to the wrist, the wearable offers a promising non-invasive tool to help individuals better manage their symptoms.
