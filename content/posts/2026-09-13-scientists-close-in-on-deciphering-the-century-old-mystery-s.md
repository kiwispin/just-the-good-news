Researchers have successfully calculated the light-absorbing properties of the mysterious substance creating dark patterns in Venus's atmosphere. This breakthrough significantly narrows down the possibilities, bringing scientists closer than ever to determining if the material is organic or inorganic. The discovery provides a crucial roadmap for future space missions to finally solve this long-standing planetary riddle.
