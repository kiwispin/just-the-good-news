Scientists have developed an ultrathin 'metacrystal' from gold film that successfully sorts and transports quantum light at room temperature. This breakthrough could eliminate the need for massive, ultra-cold refrigeration systems, paving the way for more accessible and energy-efficient quantum technologies.
