Pakistan has established a continuous wildlife sanctuary spanning 250 miles of the Indus River, securing vital habitat for the endangered Indus River Dolphin. This newly consolidated protected area will safeguard an estimated 440 dolphins, offering a critical refuge from the pressures of industrial and agricultural development.
