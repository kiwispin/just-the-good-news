Researchers have discovered that plasmalogens, a substance found in edible sea squirts, can significantly reverse signs of aging in mice. The natural compound improved memory, reduced brain inflammation, and even encouraged healthier coat growth, offering a promising new avenue for supporting healthy brain aging in humans.
