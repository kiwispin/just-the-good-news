NASA's Perseverance rover has officially set the all-time record for the longest distance driven on another world, surpassing 28 miles on the Martian surface. Powered by advanced self-driving technology, the rover achieved this milestone in just five and a half years and is currently studying ancient rocks for signs of past planetary life.
