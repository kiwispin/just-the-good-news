Engineers have successfully aligned NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope by moving a mirror just a fraction of a human hair's width. This precise fine-tuning improved the observatory's image quality by nearly 30% over original predictions, paving the way for unprecedented views of the universe.
