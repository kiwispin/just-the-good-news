New research reveals that 'pioneering pairs' of ospreys nesting away from established populations are driving the species' successful recovery across Britain. This independent nesting behavior is proving to be a key factor in boosting the population of these magnificent birds, which originally went extinct in the British Isles in 1916 before beginning their gradual return.
