An exciting new housing initiative in Maine is utilizing 3D-printing technology to construct homes using a bio-based 'ink' made from local sawmill wood residuals. This innovative approach aims to build housing more quickly and efficiently while repurposing forest byproducts that would otherwise go to waste.
