Supercomputer simulations have finally shed light on the mysterious 'Little Red Dots' captured by the James Webb Space Telescope, revealing them as rapidly growing black holes from the dawn of the universe. This breakthrough provides scientists with a crucial piece of the puzzle in understanding how the cosmos's most massive structures formed so quickly after the Big Bang.
