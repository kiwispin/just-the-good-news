Family-owned bus operator GVC has launched New York State's largest fleet of electric school buses in the South Bronx, a neighborhood historically impacted by high childhood asthma rates. The zero-emission vehicles will transport students with special needs while significantly reducing local air pollution. This initiative marks a major step forward for both community health and clean energy transportation.
