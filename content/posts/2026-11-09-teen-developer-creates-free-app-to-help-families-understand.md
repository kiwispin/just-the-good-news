Inspired by a desire to bridge communication gaps for his autistic cousin, a self-taught teenager built Kora, a machine learning app that assists parents and caregivers in interpreting emotional cues. The free tool is currently supporting more than 700 users across 12 countries, largely through partnerships with autism therapy centers.
