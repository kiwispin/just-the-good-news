After more than 150 years, wild kiwi are once again thriving in the hills surrounding New Zealand's capital city. The milestone is the result of the Capital Kiwi project, a collaborative conservation effort driven by local Māori iwi, community members, and private landowners who have reintroduced over 250 of the iconic birds.
