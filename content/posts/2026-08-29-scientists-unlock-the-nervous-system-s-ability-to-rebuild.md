Researchers have identified a protein that acts as a roadblock to nerve repair, finding that blocking it allows damaged fibers to successfully regrow. In lab studies, this breakthrough significantly improved movement and sensation after spinal cord injuries, paving the way for therapies that help neurons actively rebuild.
