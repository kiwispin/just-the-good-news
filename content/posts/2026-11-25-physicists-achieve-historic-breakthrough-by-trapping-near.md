Scientists have successfully suspended a positron in a wall-free trap and cooled it to near absolute zero, safely isolating it from ordinary matter. This landmark achievement allows researchers to conduct the most precise comparison yet between matter and antimatter, bringing us closer to understanding the fundamental nature of the universe.
