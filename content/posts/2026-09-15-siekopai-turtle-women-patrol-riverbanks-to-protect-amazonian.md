In the Amazon, a group of seven Indigenous Siekopai women known as the Tari Nomiowa’i are actively safeguarding endangered freshwater turtles by patrolling local riverbanks to locate and protect vulnerable nests. Their dedicated conservation efforts are helping these vital reptile populations recover and thrive. This community-led initiative beautifully blends traditional ecological knowledge with active habitat protection.
