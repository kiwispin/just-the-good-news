With fewer than 100 wild ocelots remaining in the United States, newly captured trail camera footage shows the endangered wildcats safely utilizing dedicated wildlife crossings in Texas. These specialized underpasses are proving crucial in preventing vehicle collisions and helping to guide the species back from the brink of extinction.
