For the first time, scientists have successfully detected a faint trail of antineutrinos lingering after a nuclear reactor has shut down. This breakthrough confirms predictions about radioactive decay and opens the door for advanced, non-invasive technology to safely monitor nuclear facilities even when they are offline.
