British Retirees Celebrate 15 Years of Opening Their Hearts and Home to 59 Foster Dogs

Good News Network 21 September 2026

Photo: Veronica Dudarev / Unsplash

Nicole and Gary Wordsworth have spent their retirement providing a loving, temporary home for dozens of rescue dogs from the Battersea Dogs Home. By choosing to foster part-time, the Buckinghamshire couple has helped 59 pups transition from kennels to caring households with no plans of slowing down.
