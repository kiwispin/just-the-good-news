Scientists are developing a cleaner recovery method inspired by sea sponges and plants to harvest valuable rare earth elements from industrial waste like coal ash. This innovative approach could significantly reduce the need for new mining, harsh chemicals, and energy-intensive processing, turning waste piles into useful resources.
