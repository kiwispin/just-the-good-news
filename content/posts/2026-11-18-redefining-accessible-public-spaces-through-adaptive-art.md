Innovator April Surac has launched NeuraSensa, a technology that allows public art installations to adapt dynamically to the sensory needs of individual visitors. By adjusting lighting, sound, and textures in real time, the platform makes cultural experiences genuinely inclusive for neurodivergent and disabled individuals.
